Hiho, I'm Felipe. I'm a Senior Software Engineer and Frontend Chapter Lead at adidas, based in Zaragoza, Spain, originally from Recife, Brazil.

This is the first post on the new blog. Nothing fancy, just a quick note on why it exists.

## Why a blog

I spend my days on micro-frontends, build tooling, cloud infrastructure and, more and more, AI-assisted engineering. Most of what I learn ends up in a pull request description, a chat thread or my head. None of those are easy to find again, and none of them help anyone else.

Writing it down is a cheap fix, and also `force`s me to clarify my thoughts. If a post saves me an hour six months from now, or saves someone else an hour today, it did its job.

## What to expect

- **Short notes** on things I ran into while building: a tricky bug, a migration, a tool that surprised me.
- **Weekly findings** when there is something worth sharing, not on a strict schedule.
- **Ideas and experiments**, including the ones that did not work out.
